Category 01

General Questions

Understanding the role of book marketing and who our services are designed to support.

A professional book marketer helps bridge the distance between a finished book and the readers most likely to appreciate it. Rather than relying on guesswork or scattered posts, a book marketer studies your book's genre, themes, and intended audience, then builds a structured plan for how the book is presented, positioned, and promoted.

In practice, this encompasses marketing strategy, book promotion, author branding, social media direction, book launch planning, promotional copywriting, reader engagement, and platform development — all working together so your book is presented professionally and given meaningful opportunities to be discovered.

Any author who takes their work seriously and wants a clearer, stronger way to connect with readers can benefit from professional book marketing. This includes authors preparing for an upcoming release, writers whose already-published books need renewed visibility, and authors who want to establish a recognisable long-term author brand.

Whether you need a complete campaign from the ground up or focused support in one specific area — such as refining your book description, structuring a launch, or organising your social media presence — thoughtful marketing brings clarity and structure to your promotional efforts.

Yes — independent and self-published authors are at the heart of David Tenny Book Marketing. Independent publishing gives writers remarkable creative freedom, but it also places the responsibility of marketing, branding, and visibility squarely on the author's shoulders.

My services are designed to give independent authors the strategic direction, creative presentation, and promotional structure needed to present their books at a high professional standard without having to navigate every marketing decision alone.

Absolutely. Launching a first book is an exciting milestone, and it is completely natural for the marketing side of publishing to feel unfamiliar at first. You do not need an existing audience or prior marketing experience to get started.

For debut authors, I focus on building clean, practical foundations — clarifying who your readers are, shaping your initial author presence, refining how your book is described, and creating a manageable plan so your debut enters the world with confidence and direction.

I work across a wide range of both fiction and nonfiction genres — including literary fiction, mystery, thriller and suspense, historical fiction, romance, speculative fiction, memoir, personal development, business, history, and poetry, among others.

Because every project begins with studying your specific genre and the expectations of its readers, the strategy, tone, and visual direction are always adapted to suit the world your book belongs to.

Writing a compelling book is a major achievement, yet publication alone does not automatically place a book in front of readers. In a crowded publishing environment, readers can only choose from the books they actually discover and recognise as meant for them.

Book marketing matters because it gives your work visibility, communicates its value clearly, builds recognition around your name as an author, and creates steady opportunities for readers to encounter, engage with, and remember your writing.

Yes. While full packages are convenient for authors looking for coordinated multi-channel support, you are always welcome to request an individual service — whether that is a standalone marketing strategy, author branding guidelines, book launch planning, or promotional copywriting.

You can explore all ten areas on the Services page and mention the specific service you are interested in when you get in touch.

Services Questions

How each area of promotion, branding, content, and launch support works in practice.

I offer ten core categories of book marketing and promotional support: Book Marketing Strategy, Book Promotion and Visibility, Author Branding, Social Media Marketing, Book Launch Marketing, Content Marketing and Copywriting, Reader Engagement, Digital Marketing Support, Author Platform Development, and Promotional Campaign Development.

Together, these cover everything from high-level positioning and launch coordination to hands-on promotional copy, campaign concepts, and long-term audience building. Full details for each category can be found on the Services page.

Yes. Author branding is one of the most valuable investments a writer can make, because readers often fall in love with an author's voice and identity across multiple books. I help you define a cohesive, authentic brand — including your positioning, brand voice, messaging themes, professional biographies, and visual direction.

The goal is never to turn you into something artificial, but to distil what makes your writing distinct so readers experience a consistent, memorable impression wherever they encounter your name.

Yes. Social media marketing support is designed to help authors maintain an engaging, professional presence without feeling overwhelmed by daily posting. Depending on your needs, this includes identifying the platforms best suited to your readership, establishing clear content pillars, building structured content calendars, writing post and caption copy, and providing creative direction for promotional visuals.

Everything is shaped around your genre and voice so your social channels feel genuine and inviting to readers.

Yes. A well-prepared book launch unfolds across three stages: pre-launch anticipation, release-week visibility, and post-launch momentum. I help you plan and coordinate each stage with a structured launch roadmap, teaser and countdown concepts, release announcements, promotional copy, and a clear schedule of activities.

Whether you are months ahead of publication or approaching your release window, structured launch support helps ensure your book's arrival is organised and impactful.

Yes. Through Content Marketing and Copywriting as well as Promotional Campaign Development, I craft the written and conceptual materials needed to present your book persuasively. This includes refined book descriptions and blurbs, author biographies, promotional hooks and taglines, email newsletter copy, social media post copy, blog or article drafts, and creative direction for promotional graphics.

Every strategy I develop is customized. No two books share the exact same voice, audience, or publishing context, which is why generic checklists rarely deliver meaningful visibility. Your marketing strategy is built around your specific book, your target readers, your genre positioning, and the channels that make the greatest sense for your goals.

Yes. Book marketing does not end after launch week. Many authors seek ongoing support to keep published titles visible, nurture reader engagement between releases, grow their author platform, or build momentum across a multi-book series. Support can be arranged for a focused campaign period or structured around longer-term promotional continuity.

Pricing Questions

How packages and custom scopes are structured around each author's requirements.

Services are priced according to the level of support, the range of deliverables, and the scope of work required for your project. Because an author seeking a single refined book description has very different requirements from an author commissioning a full book launch campaign, pricing is always aligned with the actual work involved and confirmed clearly in writing before any project begins.

Yes. I offer four structured package levels on the Pricing page: Essential Book Marketing for foundational introductory support, Author Visibility for broader ongoing promotion, Comprehensive Book Marketing for extensive multi-channel campaigns, and Custom Marketing Solutions for fully tailored requirements.

Yes — our Custom Marketing Solutions option exists specifically for authors whose needs combine elements from different areas of the catalogue. You can select the exact services that fit your book and goals, and I will prepare a tailored scope and quote around those priorities.

Yes. Pricing directly reflects the specific services, depth of strategy, volume of creative and written deliverables, and duration of support your project requires. This ensures that you only invest in the marketing activities that are genuinely relevant and helpful for your book.
