Foundation

Book Marketing Strategy

A clear, structured marketing plan built around your book — before a single promotional step is taken.

Book Marketing Strategy is the foundation beneath everything else. It is a tailored, written plan that defines who your book is for, how it should be positioned, which messages will resonate, which channels deserve your energy, and in what order your promotional efforts should unfold.

The process involves a close look at your book's genre, themes, and comparable titles; the development of detailed reader profiles; positioning and messaging that distinguish your book within its category; and a practical roadmap that turns intention into an organised sequence of action. It matters because scattered promotion exhausts authors and confuses readers — strategy replaces guesswork with direction, and makes every hour and every effort count toward a defined objective.

Possible Deliverables
  • Complete marketing strategy document
  • Target reader profiles
  • Positioning & messaging framework
  • Channel selection & priority plan
  • Promotional timeline & roadmap
  • Genre & market positioning notes
How It Fits Your Objectives

Strategy is the anchor for every other service in this catalogue — the ideal starting point for authors who want their marketing to feel coherent, purposeful, and professionally directed.

02
Visibility

Book Promotion & Visibility

Focused promotional activity designed to place your book in front of the readers most likely to love it.

Promotion and visibility work is about one essential thing: making sure your book is actually seen. Readers can only choose from the books they encounter — this service exists to make those encounters happen deliberately, across the places your intended audience already spends its attention.

This involves planning and executing promotional pushes around your book; crafting compelling promotional copy that captures its essence; identifying visibility opportunities suited to your genre; coordinating promotional timing; and building a steady rhythm of exposure rather than a single announcement that fades within days. It matters because even exceptional books remain invisible without consistent, well-placed promotion — visibility is the bridge between a finished book and a growing readership.

Possible Deliverables
  • Promotional activity plan
  • Promotional copy pack
  • Visibility opportunity research
  • Campaign timing & schedule
  • Book presentation guidance
  • Promotional channel recommendations
How It Fits Your Objectives

Ideal for published books that deserve more attention than they're currently receiving — and for authors ready to move from quiet availability to active, visible presence.

Presence

Social Media Marketing

A strategic, sustainable social presence that presents you professionally — without consuming your writing life.

Social media is where many readers now discover their next book — but only when it's approached with intention. This service builds a social media presence that reflects your identity as an author: the right platforms for your genre, a clear content direction, and a consistent posting rhythm that feels authentic rather than promotional noise.

The work involves platform selection based on where your readers actually are; the development of content pillars that give your posts variety and purpose; content calendars that remove daily guesswork; caption writing in your voice; visual content direction; and engagement approaches that turn followers into genuine readers. It matters because a quiet or inconsistent presence signals a quiet book — while a thoughtful, active presence keeps your name and your work circulating among readers week after week.

Possible Deliverables
  • Social media strategy
  • Content pillar framework
  • Monthly content calendar
  • Caption & post copy sets
  • Visual content direction
  • Engagement & growth guidelines
How It Fits Your Objectives

Essential for authors who want a recognisable, ongoing presence — whether building an audience before a launch or keeping readers engaged between books.

04
Identity

Author Branding

A cohesive, recognisable author identity — so readers remember your name, not just a single title.

Your author brand is the impression readers carry with them: the tone of your communication, the look of your materials, the feeling your name evokes. Author Branding develops that identity deliberately — defining how you present yourself across every touchpoint, from your biography and messaging to your visual direction.

The process involves uncovering what makes your voice and perspective distinct; crafting a brand voice and messaging guide; developing professional author biographies for different contexts; establishing visual direction — colour, typography, and mood — that suits your genre; and documenting it all so your presentation stays consistent everywhere readers meet you. It matters because readers follow authors, not just books: a strong brand turns one purchase into lasting readership and gives every future release a head start.

Possible Deliverables
  • Author brand guidelines
  • Brand voice & messaging guide
  • Professional author bio set
  • Visual direction board
  • Tagline & positioning lines
  • Brand consistency checklist
How It Fits Your Objectives

The right choice for authors thinking beyond one book — building a name and identity that grows in value with every title they publish.

05
Launch

Book Launch Marketing

A coordinated launch that builds anticipation before release day — and sustains momentum long after it.

A book launch is a rare moment of concentrated attention — and it rewards preparation. Book Launch Marketing plans and coordinates the full arc of your release: the pre-launch phase that builds anticipation, the launch period that announces your book with impact, and the post-launch phase that keeps the conversation alive when most campaigns go quiet.

The work involves a structured launch timeline; pre-launch teaser and countdown content; launch announcement copy and messaging; coordination of promotional activities around release; and a post-launch plan that converts early attention into ongoing visibility. It matters because a launch without structure is just a date — while a well-orchestrated launch gives your book its strongest possible entrance into the world, with every element working together rather than left to chance.

Possible Deliverables
  • Complete launch plan & timeline
  • Pre-launch anticipation content
  • Launch announcement copy
  • Release-week activity schedule
  • Post-launch momentum plan
  • Launch asset checklist
How It Fits Your Objectives

Designed for authors approaching a release date — whether a debut or a tenth title — who want their launch to feel organised, professional, and genuinely memorable.

Words That Work

Content Marketing & Copywriting

Professional writing that presents your book persuasively — in your voice, at a publishable standard.

Every piece of marketing is built from words: the description that convinces a browsing reader, the newsletter that keeps your audience close, the article that draws new readers toward your work. This service provides professional writing across all of it — crafted with the same care you'd expect for the book itself.

The work covers book descriptions and blurbs refined for impact; author biographies for every context; email newsletters and promotional sequences; blog posts and articles that build authority and discoverability; website copy; and promotional messaging for campaigns. It matters because readers judge a book by the words around it long before they read the words inside it — polished, persuasive copy is often the difference between a reader scrolling past and a reader deciding your book belongs on their shelf.

Possible Deliverables
  • Book description & blurb set
  • Author biography variations
  • Email newsletter drafts
  • Blog & article content
  • Website copywriting
  • Promotional messaging pack
How It Fits Your Objectives

For authors who want every public word about their book — from storefront description to newsletter — to read as well as the book itself.

Connection

Reader Engagement

Turning one-time readers into a loyal community that follows your work from book to book.

Visibility introduces readers to your book; engagement is what makes them stay. Reader Engagement focuses on the relationship after the first encounter — building genuine connection through conversation, storytelling, and consistent, human interaction that keeps your audience close between releases.

The work involves designing engagement content — discussion prompts, behind-the-book features, excerpts, and reader questions; developing newsletter concepts readers actually look forward to; planning community-building approaches suited to your genre; and establishing rhythms of interaction that feel natural rather than forced. It matters because an engaged reader is worth far more than a passing one: they recommend your book, return for your next release, and become the foundation of a sustainable author career.

Possible Deliverables
  • Engagement content ideas & prompts
  • Newsletter concept & structure
  • Reader community plan
  • Engagement calendar
  • Behind-the-book content series
  • Reader retention guidance
How It Fits Your Objectives

For authors who want more than sales transactions — a readership that grows warmer, larger, and more loyal with every interaction.

Digital Marketing Support

Practical, hands-on support that makes your entire online presence work harder for your books.

An author's digital presence is an ecosystem — website, storefront pages, email, social profiles, search discoverability — and it performs best when every part supports the others. Digital Marketing Support reviews and strengthens that ecosystem, so readers who encounter you anywhere online find a professional, coherent, persuasive presentation.

It matters because readers research before they buy — and a polished, unified digital presence quietly answers every question they have in your favour.

Foundation

Author Platform Development

The long-term foundation every serious author needs — a platform that works for you between books, not just during launches.

An author platform is everything a reader finds when they look for you: your website, your mailing list, your social presence, your professional presentation. Author Platform Development builds that foundation properly — creating a home base you own, independent of any single retailer or algorithm.

The work involves assessing your current platform; planning website structure and essential content; establishing or strengthening your mailing list foundation; optimising social profiles for consistency and discoverability; and creating a growth roadmap that develops your platform steadily over time. It matters because careers are built on platforms, not moments: when your next book arrives, a well-built platform means announcing it to an audience that's already there, already listening, and already yours.

Creative Direction

Promotional Campaign Development

Complete creative campaigns — concept, messaging, and rollout — built around your book's unique identity.

A campaign is more than a series of posts — it's a unified creative idea, executed with consistency across every touchpoint. Promotional Campaign Development creates complete campaign concepts for your book: a central theme, a messaging framework, creative direction, and a structured rollout plan that carries your promotion from first teaser to final push.

The work involves developing the campaign concept and creative theme; writing core messaging and variations for different contexts; directing the visual style of campaign materials; planning the asset list — graphics, copy, content pieces; and scheduling the rollout so the campaign builds, peaks, and sustains with intention. It matters because coordinated campaigns achieve what scattered promotion cannot: a recognisable, memorable push that readers notice, follow, and respond to — with every element reinforcing the same compelling idea.

  • Promotional graphics direction
