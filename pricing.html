Pricing

Marketing Support, Structured Around Your Book.

Four levels of professional marketing support — from essential foundations to comprehensive campaigns — each one shaped around the reality that no two authors need exactly the same thing.

A Thoughtful Approach

Why Marketing Requirements Differ Between Authors.

A debut novelist preparing a first launch and an established nonfiction author relaunching a catalogue do not need the same marketing — and they shouldn't pay for the same marketing either.

Every book has its own genre, audience, and objectives. Every author arrives at a different stage, with different foundations already in place. That's why the packages below are structured as levels of support rather than rigid, one-size-fits-all bundles — and why the final scope of any project is always confirmed in conversation before work begins.

Choose the level that feels closest to your needs, then reach out. Together we'll refine the scope so you're investing only in the support that genuinely serves your book.

  • Four clear package levels — from introductory support to comprehensive campaigns.
  • Every package can be adjusted to your book, genre, and publishing goals.
  • Custom solutions available when your needs fall between, or beyond, the packages.
  • Scope, deliverables, and timeline always confirmed before any work begins.

The Process

How Working Together Begins.

A simple, transparent path from first message to finished project — with the scope always agreed before anything starts.

01

Reach Out

Send a message through the contact form or by email. Tell me about your book, where you are in your publishing journey, and which package — or combination of services — has caught your attention.

02

Receive a Recommended Scope

You'll receive a clear recommendation: the services that fit your goals, the deliverables you can expect, the projected duration, and the investment — all confirmed in writing before any commitment.

03

Confirm & Begin

Once the scope feels right, work begins — with organised communication, agreed milestones, and the professional standard your book deserves from start to finish.

A note on honesty: professional marketing strengthens visibility, presentation, and reader connection — but no ethical marketer can promise specific sales figures or bestseller rankings. What you can expect here is dedicated, skilful, transparent work in service of your book.
