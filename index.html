Introduction

Where Great Books Meet Thoughtful Marketing.

A beautifully written book can still go unnoticed without the right presentation, visibility, and promotion behind it.

In today's publishing landscape, thousands of new titles appear every week. Discoverability has become one of the greatest challenges an author faces — not because readers are uninterested, but because readers can only choose from the books they actually encounter. Thoughtful marketing closes that gap.

David Tenny Book Marketing exists to help authors meet that challenge with professionalism and creativity. From building a recognisable author brand and planning a strategic book launch, to strengthening social media presence and developing promotional campaigns, every service is designed to give your work the visibility it deserves — and to present it in a way that feels true to you as an author.

The Bigger Picture

“Writing the Book Is One Achievement. Helping Readers Discover It Is Another.”

Many authors pour months — sometimes years — into their manuscript, only to discover that publication itself is the beginning of a second journey. Readers rarely stumble upon a book by accident. They discover books through visibility, recommendation, consistent presence, and professional presentation. Marketing is the craft of making those moments happen deliberately rather than hoping for them by chance.

i.

Discoverability

Readers can only love a book they can find. Strategic marketing places your work where your intended audience is actually looking.

ii.

Audience Targeting

Every genre speaks to a different reader. Understanding who your book is for shapes every effective promotional decision.

iii.

Author Recognition

A consistent, professional presence helps readers remember your name — not just a single title.

iv.

Consistent Promotion

Visibility is built through steady, organised effort over time — not a single announcement on release day.

v.

Brand Identity

A recognisable author brand gives your books a shared identity and gives readers something lasting to connect with.

vi.

Long-Term Development

The strongest author careers are built book by book, reader by reader — with marketing that grows alongside you.

Our Approach

Thoughtful Strategy. Creative Marketing. Meaningful Visibility.

Behind every service is a working philosophy — a set of principles that shape how each project is planned, presented, and carried out.

01 — Strategic Thinking

Every recommendation begins with a reason. Marketing decisions are grounded in your book's genre, audience, and objectives — never guesswork or one-size-fits-all templates.

02 — Creative Direction

Books are creative works, and their marketing should be too. Campaigns, visuals, and messaging are crafted to reflect the tone and spirit of your writing.

03 — Audience Awareness

Effective promotion starts with understanding readers — what draws them in, where they discover books, and what earns their trust.

04 — Professional Communication

Clear, prompt, and transparent communication at every stage. You'll always know what is being done, why it matters, and what comes next.

05 — Organized Execution

Ideas only matter when they're carried out well. Plans are delivered with structure, timelines, and careful coordination from start to finish.

06 — Attention to Detail

From a headline's wording to the pacing of a launch, the smallest details shape how professional — and how memorable — your marketing feels.
