My Purpose

“A good book should never go unnoticed simply because its author was never shown how to market it.”

That belief is the purpose behind David Tenny Book Marketing. Writing and marketing are two very different crafts — and it is unfair to expect every author to master both alone. Too many remarkable books reach only a fraction of the readers who would love them, simply because the promotional side of publishing felt overwhelming, unfamiliar, or out of reach.

My purpose is to close that gap: to give authors thoughtful, professional, creative marketing support that strengthens their presence, respects their voice, and gives their books a genuine opportunity to be discovered — so they can keep their energy where it belongs: on the writing.

My Marketing Philosophy

Understand the Book First. The Marketing Follows.

Effective book marketing is never improvised. It grows out of a careful understanding of each book, its genre, its intended readers, and its promotional objectives. These are the principles that guide every project I take on.

"Marketing that begins with listening will always outperform marketing that begins with noise."
i.

Understand the Book First

Before any strategy is drafted, I take time to understand the heart of the book — its genre, tone, themes, and the promise it makes to its readers. Marketing built on understanding always outperforms marketing built on assumption.

ii.

Know the Intended Reader

Effective promotion speaks to someone specific. Identifying who your readers are, where they discover new books, and what genuinely moves them shapes every decision that follows.

iii.

Respect the Genre's Language

Every genre has its own visual and verbal conventions — its own way of signalling to readers "this book is for you." Marketing should feel native to your genre while still standing apart within it.

iv.

Protect the Author's Voice

Promotion should sound like you — not like an advertisement. The most effective author marketing feels authentic, personal, and true to the writer behind the work.

v.

Favour Consistency Over Noise

A steady, well-paced presence builds recognition; loud bursts of promotion fade quickly. Sustainable visibility is built through organised, consistent effort over time.

vi.

Let Strategy Lead Creativity

Beautiful, creative marketing is most powerful when it serves a clear objective. Every visual, every message, and every campaign element should exist for a reason.
